Component registries

14 sources

The direct siblings: React + Tailwind components you install or paste, mostly shadcn-compatible. This is where 21st.dev’s actual catalogue competes.

shadcn/uiui.shadcn.com
The substrate everything else extends. Read its registry spec if you’re building anything catalogue-shaped.
Free
Magic UImagicui.design
150+ animated components. The closest thing to a direct competitor — same audience, same install flow, more polish on motion.
Free
Aceternity UIui.aceternity.com
Maximalist, motion-heavy hero and section blocks. Good for seeing how far a single component can be pushed.
Free
Origin UIoriginui.com
Enormous depth on the boring stuff — inputs, selects, dialogs, in dozens of variants each. Best-in-class form reference.
Free
Cult UIcult-ui.com
Fewer components, unusually high finish. Study the detail work — shadows, easing, layered surfaces.
Free
Kokonut UIkokonutui.com
Broad Tailwind + shadcn set with a generous free tier and clean, commercial-looking blocks.
Free
Motion Primitivesmotion-primitives.com
Animation-first primitives by ibelick. Small, composable — the opposite of “drop in a whole hero”.
Free
React Bitsreactbits.dev
Animated React components leaning into visual effects — text reveals, backgrounds, cursor tricks.
Free
Fancy Componentsfancycomponents.dev
Experimental typographic and physics-driven pieces. Not production defaults — idea fuel.
Free
Animate UIanimate-ui.com
shadcn-shaped components with motion built in rather than bolted on.
Free
Background Snippetsbg.ibelick.com
Pure-CSS page backgrounds — grids, glows, gradients. Solves a real problem in about 30 seconds.
Free
tweakcntweakcn.com
Visual theme editor for shadcn. Useful both as a tool and as a model of catalogue-plus-editor product design.
Free
Uiverseuiverse.io
Thousands of community-submitted CSS components. Quality varies wildly — the interesting part is the contribution model.
Free
Hover.devhover.dev
Animated Tailwind/React components sold as a kit. A good look at how these catalogues monetize.
Paid

Kits & primitives

9 sources

Full systems rather than à-la-carte snippets. Reach here for coverage and accessibility rather than novelty.

Tailwind Plustailwindcss.com/plus
Formerly Tailwind UI. Still the quality bar for marketing sections and application shells — worth the licence if you ship a lot of pages.
Paid
Preline UIpreline.co
Huge free Tailwind component set, framework-agnostic. Broad but generic — good bones, bring your own taste.
Free
Flowbiteflowbite.com
Tailwind components with React/Vue/Svelte bindings and a big block library.
Free
daisyUIdaisyui.com
Semantic class names on top of Tailwind, plus a genuinely good theming system worth borrowing conceptually.
Free
HyperUIhyperui.dev
Free, no-dependency Tailwind blocks — marketing, ecommerce, application. Copy-paste with zero install.
Free
HeroUIheroui.com
Formerly NextUI. Opinionated, well-animated React system with a distinct visual voice.
Free
Park UIpark-ui.com
Ark UI + Panda CSS. Interesting if you’re considering anything other than Tailwind.
Free
Base UIbase-ui.com
Unstyled, accessible primitives from the Radix and MUI teams. The foundation layer, not the look.
Free
Radix Primitivesradix-ui.com/primitives
What shadcn/ui is built on. Read the docs for behaviour specs even if you never install it.
Free

Landing & site galleries

For the look rather than the code — full-page screenshots you can scan a hundred at a time to calibrate a direction.

Godlygodly.website
The highest signal-to-noise gallery in this list. Tightly curated, heavy on developer-tool and studio sites.
Free
Land-bookland-book.com
Very large, well-tagged archive. Filterable by industry, style and type — the workhorse.
Free
SaaS Landing Pagesaaslandingpage.com
Narrow on purpose: SaaS only, broken down by section (pricing, testimonials, footers).
Free
Landingfoliolandingfolio.com
Gallery plus a component library of individual landing sections.
Free
Lapa Ninjalapa.ninja
Long-running landing page collection with free UI-kit and book sections attached.
Free
One Page Loveonepagelove.com
Single-page sites only. The constraint makes it unusually useful for structure decisions.
Free
SiteInspiresiteinspire.com
Older, more editorial and agency-leaning. A good antidote if everything you look at is starting to rhyme.
Free
Awwwardsawwwards.com
Maximalist and often impractical, but the place to see what’s technically possible this year.
Free
Minimal Galleryminimal.gallery
Restrained, typography-led sites. A useful counterweight to the motion-heavy registries.
Free
Dark Mode Designdarkmodedesign.com
Dark interfaces only. Genuinely handy — dark palettes are where most homegrown systems fall apart.
Free
Httpsterhttpster.net
Scrappier and more idiosyncratic than the rest. Good for breaking out of a rut.
Free

Product UI references

6 sources

Not marketing pages — real screens, states and flows from shipped products. This is what you want once you’re designing past the homepage.

Mobbinmobbin.com
The big one. Hundreds of thousands of screenshots from real apps, indexed by flow and element. Free tier is limited but usable.
Freemium
Referorefero.design
Web-app UI reference, searchable by component and page type. Stronger than Mobbin on desktop web.
Freemium
Page Flowspageflows.com
Recorded videos of real user flows — onboarding, upgrade, cancellation. Shows the parts screenshots miss.
Paid
UI Sourcesuisources.com
Flow breakdowns with written analysis of why each screen works.
Freemium
Screenlanescreenlane.com
Lightweight, free, updated often. Filter by pattern — empty states, paywalls, settings.
Free
Nicely Donenicelydone.club
Web app screens and flows, curated small. Quick to skim end to end.
Free

Motion & interaction craft

6 sources

Where the “why” lives. Registries give you a component; these explain the easing, timing and behaviour that make it feel expensive.

Animations on the Webanimations.dev
Emil Kowalski’s course and articles. The single best resource on why interface motion feels right or wrong.
Freemium
Motion — Examplesmotion.dev/examples
Official gallery for the library half these registries are built on. Copyable and well-commented.
Free
Codropstympanus.net/codrops
Fifteen years of demos and tutorials. Ahead of the trend cycle by roughly two years, consistently.
Free
Build UI — Recipesbuildui.com/recipes
Step-by-step rebuilds of tricky interactions, with the reasoning shown rather than just the result.
Free
UI Playbookuiplaybook.dev
Documented behaviour for common components — every state, every edge case. A spec, not a skin.
Free
Rauno — Interfacesinterfaces.rauno.me
A living list of interaction details most teams never notice. Short, dense, extremely high leverage.
Free
